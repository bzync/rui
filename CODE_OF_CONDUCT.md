# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and differing technical viewpoints.
- Give and accept feedback with empathy.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct,
trolling, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private
information are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's **Security** tab by
selecting **Report a vulnerability** and labeling the report as a code-of-conduct
matter. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest,
and take action appropriate to the circumstances. Actions may include a warning,
removal of content, temporary exclusion, or a permanent ban from project spaces.
Retaliation against reporters or participants in an investigation is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere.
